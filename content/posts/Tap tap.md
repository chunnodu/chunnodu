Title: Tap, tap, tap.
Date: 2026-11-26
Category: notes
Tags: notes

Once I saw it, I couldn’t unsee it.

Tap, tap, tap.

I was in church at a Thanksgiving service for a Nigerian wedding and had zoned out for a bit until a woman tapping her hair caught my attention.

Tap, tap, tap.

Wondering why she was doing that,  I noticed it was also happening with other women, all as if in response to an unheard symphony.
As one finished, another one sometimes all the way across the room...tap tap tap. 

Occasionally, with an additional scratch. 

Tap, tap, tap, scratch.

I had to ask my sister, why were all these people tapping their heads?
Their scalps were itching, it’s just a way to get relief.
Then why on earth were so many women wearing weaves/wigs, even when it obviously seemed so uncomfortable?

Are there any downsides to not freely being able to access your own scalp. I’d imagine it would drive me crazy at some point.

Could be a scene from a horror movie.

Tap, tap, tap.
